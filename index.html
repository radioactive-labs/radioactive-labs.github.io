The stack we build on

This is the platform. It is open source, and we maintain it in the open. It is also why a build that would normally take six months takes a few weeks.

Plutonium
Convention-driven Rails framework for full business applications, with authorization and UI built in.
FlowChat
Conversational interfaces for WhatsApp, Telegram, and USSD, with interactive flows.
ChronoForge
Durable, distributed workflows for Rails that survive restarts and retry safely.
Angarium
Outbound webhooks with signing, retries, and SSRF protection.
ActiveShrine
ActiveStorage-compatible attachments backed by Shrine.
Phlexi
Component suite for building Rails UIs in Ruby.
